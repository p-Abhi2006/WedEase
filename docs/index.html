About WedEase

WedEase was born out of a simple observation: planning an Indian wedding is magical, but organizing it with disconnected vendors and hidden charges can be exhausting.

WedEase centralizes every step of wedding planning into a cohesive platform. From discovering verified marriage halls and top-rated caterers to dynamically testing packages against your financial budget, we give couples clarity and peace of mind.

How does WedEase verify vendors? +

Every wedding vendor listed on WedEase undergoes a strict 4-step verification: business credentials validation, on-site quality inspection, past client feedback checks, and price stability agreement.

Can I customize the pre-designed packages? +

Yes, absolutely! You can choose any base package and use our Custom Package Builder to add extra catering live counters, upgrade photography to 4K cinematic film, or remove items you don't need.

How does the Budget Estimator work? +

The Budget Estimator tracks your target budget in INR against standard and custom category expenses. It provides a visual progress meter and immediate warnings if you exceed your planned allocation.

Does submitting an enquiry charge my card? +

No. In this prototype MVP, the enquiry generates a consultation reference ID. Even in production, enquiries are completely free and non-binding until you sign an agreement with the vendor.

Is WedEase available across all cities in India? +

We currently showcase curated vendors in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Kochi, with destination wedding capabilities in Goa and Rajasthan.
